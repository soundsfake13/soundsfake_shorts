# Day 32 — More trees on Earth than stars in the Milky Way
Verified 2026-11-03.
- Nature news (2015) on Crowther et al., "Mapping tree density at a global scale": ~3 trillion trees, ~422 per person, ~15 billion cut per year, number of trees has fallen by almost half since the start of agriculture (~12,000 years ago). https://www.nature.com/articles/525170a
- Gigafact fact brief: global tree estimate >3 trillion (Nature 2015, trees ≥10 cm DBH); Milky Way ~100 billion stars (ESA), NASA notes estimates of 400 billion or more. https://gigafact.org/fact-briefs/are-there-more-trees-in-canadas-boreal-forest-than-stars-in-the-milky-way
