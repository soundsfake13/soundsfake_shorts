# Day 49 — The Great Pyramid was the tallest building for ~3,800 years — sources (checked 2026-11-20)

- History.com, "The Tallest Buildings in the World Through History" — https://www.history.com/articles/tallest-buildings-in-the-world
  Great Pyramid "roughly 481 feet", completed c. 2530 BC, "endured as the tallest man-made structure for more than 3,800 years";
  first surpassed by Lincoln Cathedral, whose central spire "placed in 1311" reached ~525 ft; "a storm blew over the spire in 1548";
  the pyramid "now stands around 450 feet" due to erosion.
- Smithsonian Magazine, "Race to the Sky: The World's Tallest Buildings" — https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/race-to-the-sky-the-worlds-tallest-buildings-180983131/
  Great Pyramid c. 2550 BC, 481.4 ft pre-erosion; on top "almost four millennia"; Lincoln Cathedral 1311–1548, 525 ft; spire fell in a storm in 1548.
- World Economic Forum, "These are the world's tallest structures throughout history" — https://www.weforum.org/stories/built-environment-and-infrastructure/tallest-historical-structures/
  Great Pyramid 481 ft (147 m); unsurpassed "for thousands of years"; eroded by ~10 m.
- History Revealed, "What overtook the Great Pyramid of Giza as the tallest manmade structure?" — https://www.historyrevealed.com/feature/what-overtook-the-great-pyramid-of-giza-as-the-tallest-manmade-structure/
  "For nearly 4,000 years"; Lincoln Cathedral ~160 m, central tower and spire raised 1307–1311.
- The Lincolnite — https://thelincolnite.co.uk/?p=87330
  1311 Lincoln Cathedral beat the Great Pyramid; spire ~525 ft; collapsed 1548.

Script choices: "about 3,800 years" (sources say "more than 3,800" / "nearly 4,000"); "about 4,500 years ago" (c. 2560–2530 BC);
"about 146 m" (481 ft ≈ 146.6 m); spire "~160 m" (525 ft); storm in 1548 (one source says 1549; majority 1548).
