# Day 15 — The Eiffel Tower grows in summer (sources checked 2026-11-11)

- **The Conversation**, Federico de Isidro Gordejuela, "The Eiffel Tower gets bigger every summer – here's why" (republished on Yahoo News: https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/eiffel-tower-gets-bigger-every-091134601.html) — specialists estimate 12–15 cm of growth between the coldest winter and hottest summer days; puddled iron expands ~12×10⁻⁶ per °C; tower curves slightly away from the sun; weighs 7,300 tonnes.
- **Interesting Engineering**, "Explained: the physics that makes the Eiffel Tower grow" (https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/explained-physics-makes-eiffel-tower-150018411.html) — measured seasonal variation 12–15 cm (5–6 in); leans a fraction of a degree away from the sun.
- **Official Eiffel Tower site**, "Why does the Eiffel Tower change size?" (https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/news/history-and-culture/why-does-eiffel-tower-change-size) — heat expands the metal, cold contracts it; expansion makes the tower tilt slightly away from the sun; 330 m with antennas. (Its own wording for the summer gain is vague: "a few millimeters".)
- **Official Eiffel Tower site**, Key figures (https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/the-monument/key-figures) — metal frame 7,300 tonnes; 18,038 iron parts; 330 m.
- **The Star (Malaysia) QuickCheck**, "Is it true that the Eiffel Tower doesn't have a fixed height?" (https://thestar.com.my/news/true-or-not/2024/02/16/quickcheck-is-it-true-that-the-eiffel-tower-doesn039t-have-a-fixed-height) — rated TRUE; leans away from the sun.

Wording used: "up to 15 centimeters" (softened with "up to", winter→summer range); "leans slightly away from the sun" (no angle given); "about 7,300 tonnes of iron".
