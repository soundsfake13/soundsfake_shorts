# Day 09 — A teaspoon of neutron star weighs billions of tons (verified 2026-11-03)
- McGill University newsroom (2006): PSR J1748-2446ad spins 716 times per second (~2× a kitchen blender), ~20 miles across; a teaspoon of neutron-star matter "is thought to weigh billions of tons". https://www.mcgill.ca/newsroom/node/14444
- National Academies, "One Universe: At Home in the Cosmos": neutron star ~as dense as an atomic nucleus, ~the size of a city, more mass than the Sun, a teaspoonful weighs more than 3 billion tons. https://nap.nationalacademies.org/jhp/oneuniverse/matter_98-99.html
