1. Taller in the morning: spinal discs compress under gravity during the day and re-expand lying down; 1724 measurement by Rev Joseph Wasse of up to ~15 mm daily loss — NZ Herald (citing New Scientist) https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/ithe-big-questioni-people-are-taller-in-the-morning/Z6M4RQSTPCJH6RRTA5XPP4AVI4 ; ScienceBlog ("around a centimetre").
