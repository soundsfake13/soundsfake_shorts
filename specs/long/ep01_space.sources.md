# Ep01 — 10 Space Facts That Sound Fake (verified 2026-11-03)
1. Voyager 1 one light-day on Nov 18, 2026 (~25.9 bn km), launched Sept 5 1977, interstellar 2012, golden record, LECP turned off April 2026, Voyager 2 ~2035 — EarthSky https://earthsky.org/space/voyager-1-1-light-day-from-earth-november-17-18-2026/ ; IFLScience, Sky at Night (search results).
2. Photons take ~40,000 years from core to surface, 8 minutes to Earth (93 million mi) — NASA SVS https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/11084
3. Sun = 99.8% of Solar System mass, ~1.3 million Earths fit, core ~13.6 million K — Universe Today https://www.universetoday.com/articles/10-interesting-facts-about-the-sun
4. Olympus Mons ~22 km (~2.5× Everest), base ~600 km, about the size of Arizona, slopes 2–5° — pulse.bot summary of NASA/USGS data; NASA photojournal (search results).
5. Uranus: 84-year orbit, each pole ~42 years of sunlight then ~42 of darkness — Guinness World Records https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/499817-longest-period-of-daylight-darkness-on-any-planet-in-the-solar-system
6. Neutron star teaspoon "billions of tons"; PSR J1748-2446ad spins 716 times/s (~2× a blender), ~20 miles across, ~28,000 ly — McGill University https://www.mcgill.ca/newsroom/node/14444
7. Enceladus: 101 geysers at the south pole spraying water vapour and ice, rooted in a subsurface ocean, mapped from ~7 years of Cassini images — Space.com https://space.com/26668-saturn-moon-enceladus-geysers-ocean.html
8. Apollo footprints likely to last "many millions of years"; meteorites the main threat — Space.com https://www.space.com/35352-eugene-cernan-moon-legacy-footprints.html
9. Sgr A*: ~4 million solar masses, ~27,000 ly, first image May 12 2022 by the Event Horizon Telescope, fits within Venus's orbit, data shipped on hard drives — MIT News https://news.mit.edu/2022/first-supermassive-black-hole-sagitarrius-0512
10. Diamond rain: lab experiments (SLAC / European XFEL) formed diamonds from plastic under ice-giant-like conditions; scientists believe diamonds form and sink; magnetic-field link is a hypothesis — SLAC https://www6.slac.stanford.edu/news/2024-01-08-diamond-rain-icy-planets-offers-clues-magnetic-field-mysteries
