# Day 4 — Sharks are older than trees: sources checked (2026-10-31)

- **Earliest shark(-like) evidence ≈ 450 million years ago (Late Ordovician, scales only)**
  - Natural History Museum, London — "Shark evolution: a 450 million year timeline": https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/shark-evolution-a-450-million-year-timeline (notes they are shark-like scales, debated; sharks appear "before trees even existed")
  - BBC Wildlife / Discover Wildlife — "Are sharks older than trees?": https://www.discoverwildlife.com/prehistoric-life/are-sharks-older-than-trees
- **Earliest trees ≈ 385 million years ago (Middle Devonian)**
  - ScienceDaily (Binghamton University / Cardiff University release on Wattieza, Gilboa NY): https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2007/04/070418130435.htm
  - Discover Wildlife (above): trees "first appeared 385 million years ago"
- **First dinosaurs ≈ 230 million years ago (Late Triassic)**
  - Natural History Museum, London — "Where did dinosaurs come from?": https://nhm.ac.uk/discover/where-did-dinosaurs-come-from.html
  - USGS — "When did dinosaurs live?": https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dinosaurs/when.html

Wording choices: "about" on every date; the 450 Mya evidence is described as "shark-like scales" because NHM notes
it is debated whether they came from true sharks. Gaps (65 and 200+ million years) are simple subtraction of the above.
