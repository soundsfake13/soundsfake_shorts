# Day 54 — Honeybees dance to give directions — sources checked this run

- NC State Extension, "Honey Bee Dance Language" — https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/honey-bee-dance-language
  waggle dance communicates distance and direction; angle relative to vertical = angle to flowers relative to the sun;
  waggle-run duration increases roughly linearly with distance (2.5 s ≈ 2,625 m → ~1 s ≈ ~1 km); dance on vertical comb;
  von Frisch won the 1973 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.
- PBS NOVA, "Tales from the Hive: Communication" — https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/nova/bees/hivecomm.html
  waggle dance tells nestmates where food lies; direction measured from the line toward the sun; ~75 ms more waggle ≈ ~330 ft farther (~1.3 km/s).
- NobelPrize.org, Karl von Frisch facts — https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/1973/frisch/facts/
  1973 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, shared (1/3) with Lorenz and Tinbergen.
- Wikipedia, "Waggle dance" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waggle_dance
  angle to the right/left of the sun coded by the same angle from vertical; farther target = longer waggle phase;
  performed on a vertical plane in dark cavity nests; von Frisch among the first to translate it.

Wording choices: distance said as "about one second means roughly a kilometer" (sources give ~1.05–1.3 km/s, varies by colony);
"dark hive" per Wikipedia (cavity nests).
