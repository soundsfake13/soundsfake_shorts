# Day 03 — The immortal jellyfish (Turritopsis dohrnii)

Verified 2026-10-30.

| Claim in video | Sources |
|---|---|
| Fully grown medusa is about 4.5 mm across | AMNH, "The Immortal Jellyfish" (https://www.amnh.org/explore/news-blogs/immortal-jellyfish); Wikipedia, *Turritopsis dohrnii* (max diameter ~4.5 mm) |
| When injured, starving, stressed, sick or old it can revert to the polyp (juvenile) stage | AMNH (physical damage, starvation → "transforming back into a polyp"); Wikipedia (environmental stress, physical assault, sick or old; starvation, temperature change, low salinity in experiments) |
| Then grows up again; theoretically could repeat indefinitely | AMNH (new genetically identical jellyfish); Wikipedia ("could go on indefinitely") |
| In practice most still die — eaten or disease | Wikipedia (predation and disease, "individuals can still die"); 2022 PNAS genome study coverage (CNN, Aug 30 2022) |

Not used: "only immortal animal" (unverified superlative), specific rebirth counts.
