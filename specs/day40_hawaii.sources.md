# Day 40 — Hawaii is moving toward Japan (sources checked 2026-11-11)

- USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory, "Volcano Watch — Big Island to Japan in only 63 million years":
  https://www.usgs.gov/news/volcano-watch-big-island-japan-only-63-million-years
  — Pacific Plate moves the islands NW at ~100 mm (4 in) per year; trajectory points toward Hokkaido, ~6,300 km away;
    63 million years if motion continued unchanged, though plate motions will likely change first.
- National Geographic Education, "Chain of Islands: Hawaiian Hot Spot":
  https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/chain-islands-hawaiian-hot-spot/
  — Pacific Plate moves NW over the hot spot at roughly 7 cm (2.75 in) per year.
- USGS "Hawaiian Volcanoes" (General Interest Publication): https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/hawaii/page11.html
  — older islands have moved northwestward beyond the stationary hot spot as the Pacific Plate moves.

Wording: speed given as a range "7–10 cm a year" to cover both sources; "length of your finger" is an illustrative
comparison only; arrival stated as conditional ("if the plate never changes course").
