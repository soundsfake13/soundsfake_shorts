# Day 36 — Tomatoes were sold as medicine in the 1830s (verified 2026-11-03)
- Atlas Obscura, "Tomato pill": John Cook Bennett (1834) claimed tomatoes could prevent cholera and treat diarrhea, headaches, dyspepsia; by 1838 Archibald Miles and Guy R. Phelps sold tomato pills and feuded in newspapers ~2 years, each calling the other a fraud; analyses found no tomato — essentially laxatives. https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/tomato-pill
- Popular Science, "Ketchup was once a diarrhea cure": Bennett promoted tomatoes for indigestion/diarrhea; tomato pills marketed as pure tomato extract, later found to contain no tomato (historian Andrew F. Smith). https://www.popsci.com/science/ketchup-history-medicine/
