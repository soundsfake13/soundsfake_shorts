# Day 55 — Titan rains methane: sources checked this run (2026-11-26)

- NASA Science, "Titan: Facts" — https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/moons/titan/facts/
  clouds, rain, rivers, lakes and seas of liquid hydrocarbons (methane, ethane); "fills great lakes with liquid natural gas";
  "only place besides Earth known to have liquids on its surface"; -179 °C (-290 °F); larger than Mercury; atmosphere ~95% nitrogen.
- The Planetary Society, "Titan" — https://www.planetary.org/worlds/titan
  mostly-methane liquid fills seas, flows in rivers, rains from the sky; only other place in the Solar System with liquid on its surface; -179 °C.
- ESA, "Titan's surface" (Cassini–Huygens) — https://m.esa.int/Our_Activities/Space_Science/Cassini-Huygens/Titan_s_surface
  shorelines and river systems seen by Huygens; Cassini radar: lakes and seas of liquid hydrocarbons; ~-180 °C.
- Space.com, "Titan: Facts about Saturn's largest moon" — https://www.space.com/15257-titan-saturn-largest-moon-facts-discovery-sdcmp.html
  methane rain, methane lakes, -179 °C, larger than Mercury, Saturn's largest moon.
