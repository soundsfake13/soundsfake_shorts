# Day 7 — Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire
Verified 2026-11-03 (19:00 slot run).
- University of Oxford, *Oxford Guide 2024/25* (official PDF): "There is no clear date of foundation but teaching existed at Oxford in some form by 1096." https://eng.ox.ac.uk/media/ip5mnhmw/oxford-guide-2024-25.pdf
- University of Oxford, About page: "oldest university in the English-speaking world", "nine centuries of continuous existence" (→ "still teaching today"). https://www.ox.ac.uk/about
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, "University of Oxford": evidence of schools at Oxford by the early 12th century (consistent with the ~1096 date; the video says "by 1096", following Oxford's own wording). https://www.britannica.com/topic/University-of-Oxford
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, "Tenochtitlán": founded c. 1325 in the marshes of Lake Texcoco. https://www.britannica.com/place/Tenochtitlan
- National Geographic Education, "Aztec Civilization": Tenochtitlan founded 1325; Triple Alliance (Mexica + Texcoco + Tlacopan) formed 1428; city fell to the Spanish in August 1521. https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/aztec-civilization/
- World History Encyclopedia, "Aztec Civilization" / "Timeline: Tenochtitlan": Triple Alliance 1428; fall of Tenochtitlan 13 August 1521 (gives c. 1345 as an alternative traditional founding date — hence "around 1325"). https://www.worldhistory.org/Aztec_Civilization/
- Arithmetic: 1428 − 1096 = 332 years ("over 300 years").
