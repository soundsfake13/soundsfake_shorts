# Day 48 — Sound travels about four times faster in water than in air

Checked in this run:
- OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1, §17.2 "Speed of Sound" (Lumen Learning mirror): air 343 m/s at 20 °C, fresh water 1480 m/s at 20 °C, sea water 1540 m/s, steel (longitudinal) 5960 m/s. https://courses.lumenlearning.com/suny-osuniversityphysics/chapter/17-2-speed-of-sound
- BBC Science Focus, "How fast does sound travel through water?": about 1,493 m/s in water, "about four times faster" than in air; water is far harder to compress, which speeds sound up. https://www.sciencefocus.com/science/how-fast-does-sound-travel-through-water
- DOSITS (Discovery of Sound in the Sea, Univ. of Rhode Island): ~1520 m/s near the ocean surface, "more than 4 times faster than the speed of sound in air". https://dosits.org/people-and-sound/research-ocean-physics/how-is-sound-used-to-measure-temperature-in-the-ocean

Derived (arithmetic on the values above): 1480/343 ≈ 4.3; 1 km takes 1000/343 ≈ 2.92 s in air vs 1000/1480 ≈ 0.68 s in water (difference ≈ 2.2 s); steel 5960/343 ≈ 17.4 → "about 17×".
