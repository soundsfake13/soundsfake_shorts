# Day 17 — A planet where it rains glass sideways (HD 189733b) — sources checked 2026-11-13

- ESA / Hubble, "Hubble spots azure blue planet" (heic1312, 11 July 2013): ~63 light-years away; deep cobalt blue;
  hazy atmosphere thought to be laced with silicate particles that scatter blue light; "rains glass, sideways";
  ~7,000 km/h winds; over 1,000 °C. https://sci.esa.int/web/hubble/-/52045-hubble-spots-azure-blue-planet-heic1312
- ScienceDaily (NASA/Hubble release, 11 July 2013): 63 light-years; cobalt blue not from oceans but hazy hot
  atmosphere; high clouds laced with silicate particles; silicate condensation could form very small drops of glass;
  "possibly rains glass — sideways — in howling 4,500-mile-per-hour winds"; dayside nearly 2,000 °F (~1,090 °C).
  https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2013/07/130711102859.htm
- Space.com, "This Blue Alien Planet Is Not at All Earth-Like": about 63 light-years; rain thought to be molten glass;
  winds up to 5,400 mph (8,700 km/h); blue from silicate particles in the upper atmosphere.
  https://www.space.com/34576-blue-alien-planet-glass-rain.html
