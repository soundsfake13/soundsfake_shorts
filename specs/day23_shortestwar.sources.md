# Day 23 — The shortest war in history lasted under an hour (sources checked 2026-11-19)

Claims and where they were confirmed:
- Anglo-Zanzibar War, 27 August 1896; widely called the shortest war in history; lasted about 40 minutes / no longer than 45 minutes — Britannica.
- Ultimatum deadline 9:00 a.m.; British ships opened fire just after 9 (History Today: about 9:02); firing ceased at 9:40 → about 38 minutes — History Today.
- Cause: after the sultan died, Khalid bin Barghash declared himself sultan; under the protectorate treaty new appointments needed British approval; he refused to stand down — History Today, Britannica (Khalid seized power; Britain preferred Hamud bin Muhammed).
- Script says "about 38 minutes" and "less than an hour" (both sources agree it was under an hour; exact length is given as 38–45 minutes).

Sources:
