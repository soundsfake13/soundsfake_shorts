# Day 18 — Scotland's national animal is the unicorn (sources checked 2026-11-14)

- National Trust for Scotland — "The unicorn – Scotland's national animal": https://nts.org.uk/stories/the-unicorn-scotlands-national-animal
  (national animal; gold chains in heraldry, thought to show only Scottish kings could tame it; purity, innocence, power; two unicorn supporters before 1603, one replaced by the English lion under James VI/I)
- VisitScotland — "The unicorn, Scotland's national animal": https://www.visitscotland.com/inspiration/culture/national-animal-unicorn
  (national animal; on the royal coat of arms; purity, innocence and power in Celtic mythology; lion added after the 1603 union)
- History Hit — "The Unicorn: Scotland's Unusual National Animal": https://www.historyhit.com/the-unicorn-scotlands-unusual-national-animal/
  (on the royal arms; unicorn coins under James III in the 15th century; chained unicorn thought to show the crown taming it; lion beside unicorn from 1603)
- Wikipedia — "Unicorn (coin)": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicorn_(coin)
  (gold coin introduced under James III, circulated 1484–1525; two crowned, chained unicorns support the Scottish royal arms)
