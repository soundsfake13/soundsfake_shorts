# Day 52 — Some bamboo can grow almost a meter in a day — sources checked (2026-11-23)

- **Guinness World Records — "Fastest growing plant"** (guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/fastest-growing-plant):
  certain species of bamboo "grow at up to 91 cm (35 in) per day", i.e. 0.00003 km/h; tallest recorded about 40 m
  in the tropics, 20–30 m in Europe/USA.
- **BBC Wildlife / Discover Wildlife — "It can grow a mighty 91cm a day – and reach a staggering 30m tall"**
  (discoverwildlife.com/plant-facts/fastest-growing-plant): "some species can grow a staggering 91cm a day";
  cells between the nodes "rapidly inflate by taking up water, elongating so that long, tubular internodes form
  quickly"; the tallest species (Dendrocalamus giganteus) "can grow up to 30 metres tall".
- **Bamboo is a grass (Poaceae)** — web search results from Britannica ("bamboo", "Poaceae: grass family") and
  Foster 1921 ("Bamboo, a giant, treelike member of the grass family"); standard botanical classification.

Derived: 91 cm / 24 h ≈ 3.8 cm per hour → "nearly 4 cm every hour".
Wording kept to "some bamboo / some kinds", "almost a meter", "about 30 meters".
