# Day 33 — Wood frogs freeze solid and come back to life
- Alaskan wood frogs survived ~-18 °C, up to 218 days frozen with 100% survival; glucose as cryoprotectant (Larson et al. 2014, J. Exp. Biol.) — https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2014/07/140722164359.htm
- Up to 60% of body freezes, heart and breathing stop, glucose ~10x normal acts as antifreeze; thaw in spring — https://audubon.org/news/frogs-freeze-survive-alaskan-winter
